How it works

Three steps. No obligation at any point, and you decide whether to move forward after you see the terms.

1

Quick eligibility check

Answer a short set of questions about your company size, industry, and the type of internal material you keep. It takes a few minutes and there is nothing to prepare in advance.

2

Secure partner portal

If your profile looks like a fit, you are introduced to the data partnership program through its own secure portal. Any material you share goes directly to them, not through us.

3

Review and decide

You review the scope, the terms, and any offer on the table. You can ask questions, involve your legal counsel, or walk away. Nothing is binding until you sign.

Companies have been offered $100K+ for anonymized data. Not all companies qualify.

What kind of data is involved

These programs are interested in anonymized operational material: the written record of how skilled work actually gets done inside a real business.

Typically in scope

Standard operating procedures, internal training and onboarding material, technical documentation, project histories, scoping notes, QA checklists, and post project reviews.

Not in scope

Customer personal data, employee personal records, payment details, health information, and anything covered by a confidentiality obligation you cannot release.

Anonymization comes first

Material is expected to be stripped of names, client identifiers, and sensitive commercial details before it is reviewed. You control what is submitted and what stays internal.

Common questions

Who are you?

We are an independent referral partner. We are not the company buying or licensing data, and we are not affiliated with any AI lab as an owner or operator. Our role is to point qualified businesses toward a program and then step aside.

Does every company qualify?

No. Eligibility depends on company size, sector, and the type and volume of documentation you hold. Many companies that apply are not accepted, and we cannot influence that decision.

What does this cost?

Nothing. There is no fee to check eligibility and no fee to be introduced. If you choose to proceed, you review the program terms directly with them before anything is agreed.
