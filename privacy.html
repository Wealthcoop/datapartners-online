Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

datapartners.online is operated by Gold Standard Solutions, an independent referral partner. We are not a data buyer. We introduce businesses to companies that run AI data partnership programs. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us through our forms, by email, or during a call. That information typically includes:

  • Your name and job title
  • Your business email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your company name and approximate employee count
  • General information about the software and systems your company uses

We do not ask you to upload customer records, source code, financial statements, or any confidential company files through this website. Please do not send sensitive material to us by email.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may log basic technical details such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and the referring website. This information is used to keep the site working and to understand general traffic patterns. It is not used to build advertising profiles about you.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your questions and follow up on your inquiry
  • Determine whether your business appears to fit the general criteria of a data partnership program
  • Make an introduction to a program operator when you ask us to
  • Keep records of the introductions we have made
  • Meet legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations

If we make an introduction, we share only the details needed to start that conversation, such as your name, business contact details, and company size. The program operator then handles your information under its own privacy policy and agreements. We may receive compensation if a company we refer is approved.

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks. The only sharing we do is the referral described above, which happens at your request, plus limited sharing with service providers who help us operate, such as our website host, form provider, and email provider. Those providers may only use the information to deliver services to us.

Mobile information and text messages

Mobile information will never be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be sold. If you provide a mobile number and agree to receive messages, we may text you about your inquiry and the status of an introduction. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. After you reply STOP, we will send one confirmation message and will not text you again unless you opt back in. Reply HELP for assistance, or email us at the address below.

Email communications

You can unsubscribe from our emails by using the unsubscribe link in any message or by emailing us directly. We will still be able to reply to direct questions you send us.

Cookies

We may use basic cookies to keep the site functioning and to measure aggregate traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

Data retention

We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to support the introduction and to meet legal and accounting requirements, then we delete or anonymize them.

How to request deletion

You may ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you. Send your request to goldstandardsolutions24@gmail.com from the email address you used, or include enough detail for us to locate your record. We will confirm receipt and act on the request within a reasonable period, usually within 30 days. Note that if we have already made an introduction at your request, you will need to contact that program operator separately regarding information they hold.

You may also ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to correct it, using the same email address.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the limited information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and company representatives. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: goldstandardsolutions24@gmail.com
